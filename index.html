Shutterspace

Shutter, Snap, & Share

A collaborative mobile photo-sharing app that makes it simple for friends and family to collect photos and videos from shared experiences—vacations, events, or everyday moments—without the mess of group chats or scattered albums.

I built Shutterspace to solve the problem of fragmented photo sharing and to create a more intentional, private way for people to preserver memories together. For me, this project reflects how I approach software development: I love to identify real-world friction, design with the user in mind, and build a scalable, modern solution.
